=== CPU Examples ===

1. Intel Core i5-8250U
   - Year: 2017
   - Fixed-point: ~12,000 MIPS
   - Floating-point: ~50 GFLOPS (single precision)

2. AMD Ryzen 5 5600X
   - Year: 2020
   - Integer MOPS: ~150,000 MOPS (estimated)
   - Floating-point: ~140 GFLOPS (double precision)

3. Apple M1 Chip
   - Year: 2020
   - Integer: ~300,000 MOPS (estimate)
   - FP32: ~2.6 TFLOPS

=== Supercomputer Examples ===

1. Summit (USA, 2018)
   - Peak: 200 PFLOPS (floating point)
   - CPU + GPU architecture

2. Fugaku (Japan, 2020)
   - Peak: 442 PFLOPS (FP64)
   - Fixed-point MOPS not typically published, but sustained ~0.4 exa-ops on AI tasks

3. Frontier (USA, 2022)
   - First exascale system: >1 EFLOPS (floating point)
   - World's fastest as of 2023
